I tested my workout app with five people. I asked what they thought the app did, had them try starting a workout and finding their history, and asked what they would improve.

User 1 - Recording 24

They thought it was a workout tracker or fitness app. They created and saved a Push workout without much trouble, but were unsure whether to click the plus sign or the New Workout button. They rated the app 1/5 for difficulty. They liked the workout and said it would be good for people who are new to exercising.

User 2 - Recording 25

They thought it was a workout logger. They started a workout and found the history easily. They rated it 1/5 for difficulty and said it was self explanatory. They suggested adding a graph to show workout activity and a way to schedule future workouts. They liked that the app was simple and let them see their past workouts.

User 3 - Recording 26

They thought the app tracked workouts throughout the week. They picked a Push, Pull, or Legs workout and found the history without trouble. They rated it 1/5 for difficulty. They suggested showing which muscles each workout targets, and possibly showing how busy gym stations are. They said they would use the app to track their workouts over time.

User 4 - Recording 27

They thought it was a weekly workout routine. After starting a workout, they saw the timer but asked what to do next and needed a little help continuing. They found the history easily. They rated the app 1/5 for difficulty and said it was very easy to use. They suggested showing which muscle groups are being worked and liked that the app gave them a set of exercises to follow.

User 5 - Recording 28

They thought the app was for trying and tracking workouts. They were able to start a workout, enter weights and reps, finish the workout, and find the history. They rated the app 1/5 for difficulty. They suggested adding more workouts and more variety within each category. They said the app would be helpful for someone who does not know much about working out.

What I learned

All five people understood that the app was for tracking workouts just by looking at the home screen. Everyone was able to find their workout history, and most people said the app was easy to use. One person was unsure which button to use to create a workout, and another was unsure what to do after starting one. These are the main parts I should make clearer.

A few people wanted to see which muscles each workout targets. Other suggestions were a graph of workout activity, more exercise options, and the ability to schedule workouts ahead of time. One person also suggested showing how busy gym equipment is, but I would focus on the simpler changes first.

Based on the feedback, I would make the create workout buttons clearer and make it more obvious what to do after starting a workout. I would also add a visual showing the muscles being worked, a simple workout history graph, and more exercise options. I want to keep the app simple because that was one of the things people liked about it.
